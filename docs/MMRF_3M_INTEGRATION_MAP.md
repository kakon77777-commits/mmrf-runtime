# MMRF × 3M Integration Map

## MLF

MMRF uses MLF packages to preserve prime records, relation records, coordinate and residue signatures, provenance, presentation projections, conversion loss and fingerprints.

## MMLC

MMRF uses MMLC to execute and audit primality-certificate validation, relation derivation, aggregate generation, normalization invariants, append-only ingest transactions and authorized research computations.

## MMR

MMRF uses MMR to compare algorithms, traversal directions, machine profiles, database representations, index layouts, query plans, reproducibility, certificate size and replay cost.

## MMPF

MMPF is one producer of MMRF observations. It is not the only source.

MMRF may also ingest certified prime generators, published datasets, independent primality proving systems, mathematical experiments, and physical or computational simulations.

## Safety boundary

MMPF factorization certificates may be stored in L2 or L3 when their relationships could materially aid target-conditioned cryptanalysis. Public MMRF views should expose only research-safe projections.
